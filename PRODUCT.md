# MindElement — Product Context

## Product
**Name:** MindElement (brand name); internal project name: MindState  
**Tagline:** Sharper Every Day  
**Purpose:** An evolving suite of elite brain-training logic games — 24 games, 100 algorithmic stages each. Score-based, streak-driven, competitive.

## Register
**brand** — the landing page and marketing surfaces are conversion-focused brand experiences. The app screens (games hub, game shell, leaderboard) are product surfaces.

## Users
Three primary audiences, all using this on mobile, often in small pockets of time:
1. **The Modern Parent** — wants screen time they can justify; buys for the household
2. **The High-Performing Professional** — 3-minute cognitive reset between meetings; values precision
3. **The Active Senior** — keeping recall sharp; values accessibility and no-stress play

## Brand Voice
Surgical. Competitive. No filler. The brand speaks like a coach who respects your intelligence.

Anti-patterns in copy: "crafted to spark", "step into an elegant world", vague inspirational padding.

## Anti-References (visual)
- Purple gradients
- Generic white SaaS backgrounds
- Pastel color schemes
- Bouncy rounded-corner icon grids
- Gradient text (absolute ban)
- Glassmorphic cards as default

## Strategic Principles
- Free users get 5 daily sessions; Pro is $2/month individual, $5 family-3, $10 family-7
- XP decays in real time — speed is rewarded
- Seed-based boards — every stage is reproducible, sharable
- PWA — offline play, home screen install
- No ads, ever
